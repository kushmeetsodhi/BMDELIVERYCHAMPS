Current inventory

Upload today's stock (ERP "Item Report" works as-is). Replaces the whole Stock tab.

  • Needs: Product ID, Inventory in Hand (pieces)
  • Optional: Item Name, Make, Case size

GRN (stock received)

Upload the day's GRN export. New lines are added; a line already in the sheet (same date, GRN no., product, batch, qty) is skipped, so uploading the same file twice is safe.

  • Needs: Unloading Date, Product Id, Bill Qty, Rate per Pc or Taxable Value, Expiry Date
  • Recommended: Document No., Batch, Manufacturing Date, GST Rate_Input

Daily sales

Upload pieces sold per product per day. Re-uploading a day replaces that day. Used for months not yet synced from Sales Mix.

  • Needs: Date, Product ID, Qty Sold (pcs); optional Revenue
  • Several rows for the same product and day are added together

Monthly sales from Sales Mix

Pulls pieces sold and revenue per product per month (Daily_Item) and margin % (Margins) from the Sales Mix dashboard sheet. Click after each new sales import there.
